Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026.

Muse YouTube Uploader ("the App") is a personal tool used by its owner to upload videos to their own YouTube channel. This policy explains what data the App accesses and how it is used.

Data the App accesses

When the owner signs in with Google, the App receives access to their YouTube account under the scopes youtube.upload and youtube.readonly. This lets the App upload videos to the owner's channel, read the channel's own video list, and read basic channel information (title, subscriber and video counts).

How the data is used

The data is used only to upload videos the owner chooses, and to confirm the upload status on the owner's own channel. The App does not read, collect, or store the contents of any other account, and it does not post, share, or sell any data to any third party.

Storage

Authorization tokens are stored in the owner's own secure credential store and are used only to call the YouTube Data API on the owner's behalf. Video files are sent directly to YouTube; the App does not keep copies of uploaded videos.

Data removal

The owner can revoke the App's access at any time from their Google Account settings (Security → Third-party access). Revoking access immediately stops the App from calling the YouTube API.
